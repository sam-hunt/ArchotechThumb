Archotech Thumb

[h1]About[/h1]
Adds an archotech thumb, a tiny prosthetic that conceals an archotech subpersona and an uplink to a derelict orbital weapon platform.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Install the archotech thumb via surgery to grant the orbital beam strike ability, a callable orbital power beam
[*]Cooldown between strikes is configurable in mod settings
[*]Like other archotech parts, it looks and feels natural, resists damage, and repairs itself over time
[*]Slotted into vanilla and DLC archotech reward pools alongside the archotech eye, arm, and leg
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Requires Harmony. No other dependencies.
